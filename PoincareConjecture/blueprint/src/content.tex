\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This is a custom blueprint for the Poincare conjecture. It reorganizes the
mathematical arguments according to their logical dependencies, grouping
statements by their role in the proof and retaining the definitions and results
needed for the final argument. Standard prerequisites outside the main route
are recorded as precise, source-linked interfaces.

The proof is organized in five stages. Chapter 1 establishes the analytic
comparison tools and the topology of necks and caps. Chapter 2 constructs and
controls Ricci flow with surgery. Chapter 3 develops the curve-shrinking
estimates used to measure surviving topology. Chapter 4 turns those estimates
into finite extinction. Chapter 5 reconstructs the initial manifold across the
surgeries and completes the topological argument. The dependency graph records
the logical flow between these stages and ends at the Poincare conjecture as its
unique terminal statement.
